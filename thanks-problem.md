The most common reasons:

- **The message was empty.** Questions, ideas and praise need a few words.
- **The e-mail address had a typo.** You can also leave it empty.
- **Several messages were sent today already** from your connection. Please try again tomorrow.

Please go back, check the form and send it again: your browser usually keeps what you typed.
