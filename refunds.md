All orders are processed by our reseller **Paddle.com**, which also handles refunds.

- **First payment:** if the API is not right for you, ask for a refund within **14 days** of your first payment and
  you get the full amount back, no questions asked.
- **Renewals:** a monthly renewal can be refunded within 14 days if the key was not used in that month.
- **Overage charges** for usage that already happened are not refundable, unless they were caused by an error on our
  side.
- **Service problems:** if an outage or a bug on our side made the API unusable for you, write to us and we refund
  the affected period.
- **Cancelling** stops future renewals; the current month stays active until it ends.

**How to ask:** reply to your Paddle receipt, use the link in it, or write to
{% include email.html subject="Refund request" %} with the e-mail address you used at checkout. Paddle returns the
money to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.

This policy does not limit your statutory rights as a consumer, including the EU right of withdrawal.
