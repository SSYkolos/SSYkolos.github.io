01

What it does

  1. Complete data, not just what the card shows

    The extension reads the data behind the result list, so phone, website and full address are exported even when a result card does not display them.

  2. Falls back by itself

    If Google changes its data format, the extension switches to reading the result cards and says so in the side panel.

  3. No duplicates

    Leads are matched by Google's place ID, so repeated or overlapping searches merge cleanly.

  4. Emails and social profiles

    Visits each business website (homepage, contact and imprint pages) to find public email addresses, including Cloudflare-protected ones, plus LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube and TikTok profiles.

  5. Multi-search queue

    Paste a list of searches, for example one per district, to get past the roughly 120 results Google Maps shows per search. (Pro)

  6. Excel-ready export

    UTF-8 CSV with correct accents, an optional semicolon delimiter for European Excel, and phone numbers kept as text.

05

Known limits

What it does not do yet, so you can decide before you integrate.

  • Google Maps shows about 120 results per search, and fewer when you are not signed in to Google. Multi-search is the way around it.
  • Keep the Google Maps tab visible while extracting. Chrome pauses background tabs.
  • Google can change its page at any time. The extension falls back to the result cards, but a larger change may need an update.
  • Contact details can be personal data. You are responsible for using them lawfully, for example under the GDPR and the anti-spam rules for cold email in your country.

06

Privacy

The extension works entirely inside your browser. There is no server that receives your data, no user account, and the leads you extract are never seen, collected, sold or shared by us.
