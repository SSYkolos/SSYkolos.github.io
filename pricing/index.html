How it works

  1. Click Buy. Paddle's secure checkout opens; it shows the final price with the VAT or sales tax for your country.
  2. After payment, your API key arrives by e-mail from support@ssydynamics.dev within a minute.
  3. Send it in the X-API-Key header. The product pages show every endpoint and an example.

Paddle.com is the merchant of record for all orders: it handles payment, invoices, VAT and refunds. Usage above the monthly quota is charged at the start of the next month. See the terms and the refund policy. Questions before you buy? {% include email.html %}.
